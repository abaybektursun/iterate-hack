# Tier list of the project's novel findings (2026-11-15)

How it was made (board result-8c92fc): 22 agents read every study, the board, the theory rounds, the site, `meta/` and the git log and returned 217 candidates; one agent merged them into 144 distinct findings; the 30 most promising were each re-read at their artifacts by one agent and checked against the literature by another; five judges (frontier scientist, interpretability researcher, practitioner, newcomer, founder) tiered all 30. The score is the mean of the five tiers (S = 5 ... D = 1); tier S from 4.5, A from 3.8, B from 2.9, C from 1.8. The top 10 were checked a second time with web search, because `EXA_API_KEY` was not set on the laptop and 24 of the 30 first checks used the stored paper notes only. The other 114 findings were not re-read at their artifacts; five judges picked the best of them from the claims alone (last section).

Full dossiers (claim after the check, artifact paths, weakest point, closest prior work, each judge's reason): `round1.json`; web novelty checks and the second look: `round2.json`.

The finding in tier S became the paper `studies/rlneurons/paper/the-finish-dial.pdf` (https://monitor.invariant.team/paper/the-finish-dial.pdf).
