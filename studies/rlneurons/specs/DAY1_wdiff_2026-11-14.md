## Merge (`jobs/merge_weights.py`)

On the GPU box, before `run_cell`: base and the other model are read from the `iterate-hf-cache` Volume tensor by tensor,
computed in float32, rounded to bf16 and written to `/tmp/theta` (container disk; one safetensors file per base shard; json
files from the RL model). The job writes `$OUT_DIR/merge.json`: per tensor numel, changed entries, norms, |delta| in bf16 units
in the last place (ulp) of the base value; sums per tensor group and layer; the effective a = <M - B, delta> / ||delta||^2 and
||M - B|| / ||delta|| of the written model.

Rounding. An RL update is often one bf16 ulp of the weight. Rounding base + a * delta to the nearest bf16 then moves a = 0.25
back to the base and a = 0.75 to RL on those entries (shown on planted one-ulp steps in `jobs/merge_weights_test.py`).
Stochastic rounding keeps the expected value but adds noise. Rule, fixed now: a statistics-only job (`merge_weights --other rl`
and `--other code`, no weights written; it simulates both roundings for a in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.25, 1.5} and the random
control) runs first. Line points use nearest rounding if its effective a is within 0.03 of a at every one of those a, otherwise
stochastic rounding. The random control uses the rounding whose ||M - B|| / ||RL - B|| after rounding is closer to 1. a = 1
is exact under either rounding (asserted). The choice and the effective a of every cell are reported.

Outcome of the rule (2026-11-14 23:58 UTC, job `abaybektursun-1114-152416-de10`, `merge_rl.json` `points`, before any cell):
nearest rounding gives effective a 0.122 at a = 0.25 and 0.877 at a = 0.75 (both off by more than 0.03), so line points use
stochastic rounding (effective a 0.250, 0.500, 0.750, 1.250, 1.500). For the random control ||M - B|| / ||RL - B|| after rounding
is 1.018 with nearest and 1.388 with stochastic rounding, so it uses nearest.

Deviation (23:55 UTC): the merge command starts with `rm -rf /tmp/theta`. Modal reused the container of the failed first check
(`32f3`, identity assertion: `b + (o - b)` is not exact in float32, fixed to `(1 - a) b + a o` in `a112836`), and the second
check (`e931`) stopped on the existing directory.
