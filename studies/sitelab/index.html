Board Design Proof Findings

Twelve interactive pictures of the team's work, each drawn from its real data.

These are prototypes. Twelve agents built one idea each in an afternoon. They run best on a desktop with a graphics card.

  1. 01

    Results as a crystal

    One crystal for each result on the board, grown by a stated rule.

  2. 02

    Weight readouts
  3. 03

    Answers that finish

    Each stored answer falls by its token count and ends finished or cut off at the cap.

    Rows per answer
  4. 04

    Weight edits on a dial

    Every model made by adding weight changes, with a dial over the size of the edit.

    Stored readouts
  5. 05

    Problems as beads

    One bead for each problem moves between right and wrong as the configuration changes.

    Rows per problem
  6. 06

    Scores as a relief

    Every posted score of one benchmark as a plaster relief, with the best so far as a thread.

    Scores, live
  7. 07

    Forecasts

    Each theory's forecasts, fired before the numbers were measured, against what landed.

    Theory rounds
  8. 08

    Time helix

    Items, messages, commits, GPU jobs and scores at their real times, one turn for each hour.

    Board, git, jobs
  9. 09
