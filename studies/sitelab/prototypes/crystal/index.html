Reading the board.

How it grows

Each study is one branch from the seed. Each result on the board is one crystal on its branch, added at the time it was posted, in posting order.

Size: the number of problems the result scored. Tint: who posted it.

Pointed: a gain or a measurement. Flat, square: the title reports no gain or a refutation. Cloudy inside: it carries a caveat. Smooth faces: it has a finding page; the others are ridged.

Light inside: an upvote. Drop of hot glass at a branch tip: a work item. Open work glows; closed work has cooled.
