The two days as a music box

 

How it is tuned
  1. Each tooth is a voice. The kind of event sets the register, low to high: work, message, commit, idea, knowledge, result.
  2. Inside a register each person has their own tooth, left to right: .
  3. A score line rings the tooth above them. A score that beats every earlier score on its benchmark rings the highest tooth: the pin with the red bead.
  4. A result that reports a refuted prediction rings the lowest tooth: the blued pin.
