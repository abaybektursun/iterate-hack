## account-3

The outcome reward does not make those directions: an LM-judge RL run concentrates its change just as much (top-8 share 0.127 vs 0.135), Think-SFT's imitation change reads the same inputs (4.8x chance), and Code-RL's change lies in them 5x more than Think-SFT's, so on-policy RL on step-by-step text strengthens a deliberative mode the base already runs, one mode shared by Math and Code.
