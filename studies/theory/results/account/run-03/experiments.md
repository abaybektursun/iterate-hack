# Open experiments of run-03

Separation: the log gain in nats of the proposing side over the other side if the proposer's value is measured (normal densities, sd = (high - low) / 3.29, clipped to 3). Gain over best: the predicted count minus the best count of right answers the untrained base has before on the same set, cap and grader.

### 3. r7-account:theory:intervention — gain over best +4, separation -0.06 nats, 30 GPU min (-0.002 nats per minute)
- Pair r7-account (theory state: survives weak: duel state loses to rival (-5.16 nats over 3 replicated-or-computed separating tests, the script implementing the; duel: loses to rival). Proposed by r7-account: The problems an RL-side model gets right and the cued Olmo 3 base gets wrong split into five mechanisms, applied in this order. (1) Loop: the base runs to the 16,384-token cap inside a rule-A repeat. This is a sampler artifact of T 0.6 / top-p 0.95, where top-p locks repeats. It cannot fire at T 1.0
- Run: Adaptive vote over up to 8 cued-base samples instead of 4. Start from the 4 stored samples per problem (attempt 0, samples 0-3, studies/rlneurons/results/day1_3/math500_base_cue-01/rows.jsonl, field extracted_v3). A problem is contested if the plurality extracted_v3 answer among its non-cap-hit samples has at most 2 of the 4 votes (this includes problems with no finished answer). Only for contested problems, draw 4 new cued-base samples (samples 4-7) in the same setting, with no restarts. Final answer = plurality of extracted_v3 over all available non-cap-hit samples (4 on uncontested problems, 8 on contested ones). Ties go to the answer of the shortest finished sample (fewest n_new) among the tied answers. All choices are label-free and fixed before sampling. Model allenai/Olmo-3-1025-7B @ a81bae42 (base); set MATH-500; prompt the standard base prompt with the 3 prefilled cue tokens [1584, 2268, 33413], as in job abaybektursun-1114-153825-208b; sampling T 1.0 / top-p 1.0; cap 16384; grader v3; samples 8; seeds samples 0-3: the stored seeds of job 153825-208b (attempt 0); new samples 4-7: seed 4,000,000 + 1,000*sample + problem_index. Count: Right answers over all 500 MATH-500 problems: the voted answer is graded with grader v3 against the gold. Cap-hit samples cast no vote. A problem with no finished sample among those available counts as wrong.
- Unit: right of 500
- r7-account predicts 457 [449, 465]: The mechanism says the largest open part at T 1.0, deliberation, consists mostly of problems that some cued-base samples still solve (pass@4 467 against vote@4 446), and that the base-only route to it is selection among more cued samples. Spending 4 more draws only on contested problems should move the vote toward the oracle, roughly as Wang's majority@4 to @8 went (+2.5 points), giving about 457.
- r7-account-rival predicts 457 [450, 464]: Under one difficulty axis plus sampling, the contested problems are mid-difficulty items where right and wrong answers split, so 4 more draws only cut sampling noise: the honest 4-vote of 446 rises about as far as a full 8-vote would (Wang's maj@4 to maj@8 is about +2.5 points, roughly 458). The post-hoc tie_short gain already counted in 453 is partly given back, and the result stays well below pass@4 of 467 because problems where the base solves 0 of 4 do not move.
- Best known base-only count: 453 (result-cbe0bc: base + cue, T 1.0 / top-p 1.0, vote over the 4 stored samples with ties broken by the shortest finished sample (rule chosen on this set; the honest vote after restart is 450, result-e78dfe)); gain over it +4 by r7-account, +4 by r7-account-rival
