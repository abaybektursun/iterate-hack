- earlier.json: the accounts already written by other writers about the same facts (their sentences, their three
  consequences with statistics and ranges, and their denials). Read them before writing yours.

Your account must differ from the earlier ones where the facts allow it. Deny at least $n of the earlier accounts'
consequences: pick consequences whose claims you think are false, and for each say why and what your account predicts
instead, as a range on the SAME statistic, rows and files, disjoint from the denied range. The denied consequence's
test decides the denial; a denial wins only when the measured 90 % interval lies inside your range and excludes the
adversary's value of that test (see 4). Your own three consequences must not use the same set of files as a
consequence you deny (one measurement, one credit). Add to account.json:
"denials": [{"denies": "account-1/c2", "why": "<which part of the earlier claim is wrong>", "instead": "<what your account
predicts and from which sentence>", "range": [low, high]}, ...]
